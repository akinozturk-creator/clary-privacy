How do I cancel Clary Plus?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Clary AI Focus and tap Cancel. You keep Plus until the end of the current period.

I bought Plus but it isn't unlocked

Open the Plus screen in the App and tap Restore. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID you used to buy it. If it still doesn't work, email us.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my account?

In the App, go to Profile → Account → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and synced data. Cancel any active subscription separately in iOS Settings.

My data didn't sync to my new phone

Sign in with the same method you used before (Apple, Google or email). Routines, tasks and mood history sync to your account. Cycle tracking, journal and sleep data stay on the original device by design.

Sounds stop when I lock my phone

Check that your phone isn't in Low Power Mode and that Clary's volume isn't muted in Control Center. Sounds keep playing in silent mode and with the screen locked.
