Privacy Policy

Clary AI Focus · Last updated: October 23, 2026

Clary AI Focus ("Clary", "the App", "we") is a focus and self-care app for people who find it hard to start and stay on track. It is developed and operated by Emin Akın Öztürk (sole proprietor, Türkiye), who is responsible for your data. This policy explains what we collect, why, who processes it, and how to delete it.

The short version

  • We collect only what the App needs to work: your account, the routine data you choose to sync, and basic, pseudonymous usage analytics.
  • Some of your most personal data never leaves your phone: cycle tracking, journal and reflection entries, and sleep sessions stay on your device.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites.
  • You can delete your account and synced data at any time from inside the App.

What stays on your device

Cycle and period tracking, journal and reflection entries, and sleep tracking sessions are stored only on your phone. They are not uploaded to our servers or sent to analytics. Deleting the App removes them.

AI assistant

When you ask the assistant to break down a task, the text you typed is sent through our server to Anthropic, the provider of the Claude AI model, to generate the steps. We do not store this text, and we do not send it to analytics. Anthropic processes it under its commercial terms to return the response.

Service providers

We use a small number of providers who process data on our behalf, only to run the App:

  • Supabase: account, database and file hosting.
  • PostHog (EU region): pseudonymous product analytics.
  • RevenueCat: subscription management for Clary Plus.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, in-app purchases and notifications.
  • Google: only if you choose Sign in with Google.
  • Anthropic: AI assistant responses, as described above.

We do not sell personal data or share it with anyone for advertising. We do not use advertising identifiers and do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Security

All data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Database access rules ensure your records can only be read by your own account.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the App under Profile → Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and all synced data from our servers immediately, and we ask our analytics and subscription providers to delete the records linked to your account. Your Apple subscription is managed by Apple: to stop future charges, cancel it in iOS Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or Türkiye's KVKK), you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict its processing. Most of this you can do directly in the App. For anything else, email us and we will respond within 30 days. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Clary is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. For significant changes we will also let you know in the App.
