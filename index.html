Privacy Policy

Clary AI Focus — Last updated: July 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Clary AI Focus ("the App", "we") collects, uses, and protects your information. The App is an ADHD-friendly focus and self-care application developed by Emin Akın Öztürk.

Information We Collect

Account information. When you create an account, we collect your email address and, if you sign in with Apple or Google, the name and email address shared by that provider. Sign in with Apple lets you hide your real email address.

App activity data. To sync your progress across sessions, we store the data you create in the App, such as daily routines, completed tasks, focus timer sessions, and mood or self-care entries.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers, or any data for advertising or tracking purposes.

How We Use Your Information

Your data is used solely to provide the App's functionality: signing you in, syncing your routines and focus history between sessions and devices, and restoring your data if you reinstall the App. We do not sell your data, show ads, or share your information with third parties for marketing.

Where Your Data Is Stored

Data is stored securely on Supabase infrastructure over encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) connections. Access is protected by industry-standard authentication, and your records are only accessible from your own account.

Data Retention and Deletion

Your data is retained while your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us at the email below; deletion requests are completed within 30 days.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date on this page.
